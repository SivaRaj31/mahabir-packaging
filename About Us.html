Approved Packaging Vendor for Leading Automotive & Industrial Brands

Mahabir Packaging is a leading manufacturer and supplier of a wide range of packing boxes in Chennai and across India. Established in 1971, we have built and maintained a strong base of wide clientele nationwide.

We possess a state-of-the-art manufacturing plant equipped with the latest technology for computerized design, high-precision cutting, furrowing, and adhesive processes. This empowers us to fabricate high-grade paper and corrugated boxes tailored to strict client specifications.

Selection of high-grade raw paper
Adhesive strength & warp testing
Bursting & tear resistance testing

Strict Quality Control & Diverse Applications

Our wide range of packing boxes include Duplex Cartons, Pharmaceutical Cartons, Automobile Packaging Boxes, Printed Packaging Boxes, Agriculture Packaging, Pilferage Proof Corrugated Boxes, Plastic Corrugated Boxes, Wooden Pallets & Plywood Pallets, and Corrugated Cardboard Boxes.

These solutions cater to diverse industries such as fresh fruits & vegetables, footwear, pesticides, pet bottles, pharmaceuticals, ceramic tiles, electronics, and automotive manufacturing. All raw materials are sourced from renowned suppliers and pass through rigorous in-house quality checks for dimensional accuracy, heavy load-bearing capacity, printing fidelity, and environmental durability.
