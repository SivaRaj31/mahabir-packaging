Welcome to Mahabir Packaging

Approved Packaging Vendor for Many Car Companies in India

Mahabir Packaging is a leading manufacturer and supplier of a wide range of corrugated boxes in Chennai and South India. We are a reputed company in the packaging industry since 1971. We have grown and maintained our strong base of wide clientele across India.

We possess a manufacturing plant with the latest technology for computer-supported design, cutting, furrowing and adhesive processes, empowering us to fabricate high-quality packing and corrugated boxes from high-grade paper and glues.

Our wide range of boxes are subjected to strict quality checks, starting from the selection and processing of the paper, adhesive strength and warp test to designing, cutting, printing alignment, etc., so that it characterizes lightweight, resistance to tear, bursting and splitting and accurate dimension.
